Privacy Policy: Pickleball Score

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Pickleball Score ("the app") is a scorekeeping app for pickleball, published by Prondi Teknoloji Anonim Şirketi ("we").

What stays on your device

Scores, game settings, game history, team names, the team color theme and your Pro status are stored on your device only. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. We do not receive this data.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your IP address, device and advertising identifiers, diagnostic and performance information, and how you interact with ads. This data is handled by Google under Google's own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice later. Ads are never shown while a game is being scored.

On iPhone and iPad, the app asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency before Google may use your device's advertising identifier to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the identifier is not shared and ads may be less relevant. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Buying Pro removes ads. Pro owners do not load ads.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Payment details never reach the app or us. The app only learns from the store whether the purchase exists, and keeps that status on your device. Use "Restore purchases" in the app to recover Pro on a new device.

Other data

The app can keep your screen on during a game. This is a local device setting and sends nothing anywhere. We do not use our own analytics, we do not sell personal data, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts or photos.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
