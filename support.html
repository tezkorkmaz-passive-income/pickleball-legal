Support: Pickleball Score

Need help or have an idea? Write to okant@prondi.com. Please include your phone model and app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

Common questions

  • How do I restore Pro on a new phone? Open the Pro screen (from Settings, or the Go Pro button on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.
  • Why do I see ads? The free version shows ads on the home screen, on the game-over screen, and in Settings. There are no ads during a game. Pro removes all ads.
  • Can I resume a game I closed? Yes. Unfinished games appear under "In progress" on the home screen.
  • Does the app need an account or internet? No account. Scoring works offline; only ads and purchases use the internet.

Pickleball Score is an independent app, not affiliated with any pickleball governing body.
